Handy Renos Winnipeg

We are jacks of all trades, masters of some.
We’re a Latinx-owned construction and renovation company based in Winnipeg, built for the work that isn’t simple fixes. We solve complex property problems through structural understanding, technical precision, and coordinated multi-trade execution.

With over 30 years of combined experience, we bring together structural expertise, door and window systems knowledge, and interior renovation capability to deliver work that is practical, efficient, and built for long-term performance.

We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and investors across Winnipeg and surrounding areas. Bilingual service is available in English and Spanish.

Why us?

Most renovation issues are not single-trade problems. They involve structure, materials, installation quality, and how systems interact over time. At Handy Renos Winnipeg, we are built for that kind of work.

We combine experience across structural construction, doors and windows, interior finishing, plumbing, electrical troubleshooting, and general repairs. This allows us to properly diagnose issues instead of treating symptoms.

Clients choose us because:

  1. We focus on correct solutions, not temporary fixes
  2. We understand how multiple building systems interact
  3. We can handle complex, multi-trade projects in one coordinated team
  4. We provide honest assessments before any work begins
  5. We prioritize durability and long-term value
  6. We communicate clearly throughout the project
  7. We are a practical, technically grounded team built for real-world construction challenges
